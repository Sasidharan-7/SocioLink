Transparent Civic Governance

How JanSolve Works

A closed-loop AI-driven pipeline from citizen reporting to government verification, crowd solutioning, and verified ground impact.

1

Citizen Reports

Citizens lodge issues with location, details, and optional photo evidence.

2

AI Analyzes

NLP models predict category, severity, and identify similar or duplicate issues.

3

Gov Verifies

Authorities validate authentic complaints and route them to relevant line departments.

4

Students Innovate

Matched universities and experts submit costed, feasible engineering solutions.

5

Public Votes & Impact

Citizens vote on proposals. Officials select winners, execute, and publish audit metrics.
